A New Standard

A New Standard for Workplace Wellbeing

Most organisations say wellbeing matters. Very few can demonstrate it.

Well-Being Approved provides an independent certification that helps organisations evidence their commitment to psychologically safe workplaces through structured assessment, evidence review and confidential employee verification.

Purpose

Why We Created Well-Being Approved

Workplace wellbeing has become an increasingly important conversation, yet organisations often struggle to demonstrate the culture their employees genuinely experience.

Well-Being Approved exists to provide an independent benchmark that helps organisations evidence healthy workplace culture, build trust and demonstrate accountability.

Key Pillars

What Makes Us Different

Independent

An independent certification standard, not a consultancy or employee benefit service.

Evidence Based

Assessment supported by verified documentation rather than policy statements alone.

Employee Verified

Random confidential employee verification provides genuine credibility and confidence.

Annual Review

Certification is reviewed annually to ensure ongoing commitment and maintain high standards.
